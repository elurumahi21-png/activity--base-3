House rules

These keep the building safe and quiet for everyone.

Timings

  • Main gate closes at 10:30 PM. Tell the warden in advance if you will be late.
  • Quiet hours: 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
  • Visitors are allowed in the lobby between 10 AM and 7 PM only.

Not allowed

  • Smoking, alcohol and drugs anywhere on the premises.
  • Cooking in rooms and using heaters, induction stoves or irons.
  • Loud music, parties and overnight guests.

Payments and notice

  • Rent is due by the 5th of every month.
  • Give 30 days’ written notice before moving out.
  • Damage to furniture or fittings is charged at repair cost.

Complaints and support

Tell us what needs fixing. We acknowledge every complaint the same day.

  1. Submit the form or message the warden on WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm within 2 hours between 8 AM and 9 PM.
  3. Fix time: electrical, plumbing and Wi-Fi in 24 hours; furniture in 3 days.
  4. Not resolved? Write to the owner at the email below.
